What the bundle actually runs

Lifecycle

178 no-socket commands cover join, all 13 deals, pause/unpause, chat, spectator grant/revoke, disconnect/reconnect, post-game, reset, close, and exact replay.

Devices

A separate registered micro-scenario uses three players, five device authorities, four replicas, duplicate/reordered delivery, membership change, snapshot plus tail, and convergence checks.

Audit and recovery

An off-suit action is initially unprovable. A later public club produces a delayed finding; a kick vote records the accused player’s rejection but excludes it from the two eligible approvals.

Presentation

The bundle emits semantic HTML, integer source/destination coordinates, score-sheet text interpreted back into typed scores, the Alloy witness view, and one machine-readable receipt.

Exact limits

This command composes registered fixtures and reducers; it is not one atomic live network execution. It opens no native window, browser connection, Veilid route, GPU frame, or external relay. The Alloy witness is bounded to layout-micro-2p-3v-8c-7z-7cells-8slots-int5 and is deliberately invalid. The published HTML ships no room secret, invite proof, private key, or live authority endpoint.
